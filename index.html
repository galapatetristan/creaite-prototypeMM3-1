About CreAIte

CreAIte helps students, freelancers, and SMEs create consistent, professional content quickly — designs, copy, website layouts, and video edits in one guided workflow.

Mission

Democratize professional-quality content creation by empowering students and SMEs with an intuitive, cloud-based, AI-powered platform.

Vision

Become a leading platform for AI-driven marketing content creation recognized for innovation, user-centric design, and real-world impact.

Who it’s for

Students SMEs Freelancers Creators Teams

Use CreAIte for academic outputs, business promotions, content planning, brand-consistent campaigns, website drafts, and quick video edits.

How it works

A structured workflow — from prompt to export — that keeps you in control while AI accelerates the heavy lifting.

  1. 1

    Sign in & choose a goal

    Pick a service (social, ads, web/UI, video), or type your own prompt.

  2. 2

    Brand kit applied

    Use your logo, colors, fonts, and tone for consistent outputs across all content types.

  3. 3

    Generate variations

    Get multiple layouts, captions, sections, and concepts — ready to refine.

  4. 4

    Edit & refine

    Tweak text, layout, and direction until it fits your brand and audience.

  5. 5

    Export & share

    Simulated export in this demo. In production: PNG/JPG/PDF/MP4 + links + version history.
